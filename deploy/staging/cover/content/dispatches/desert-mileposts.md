---
title: Mileposts in the High Desert
date: 2026-08-11
location: Mojave Basin
description: Heat, distance, and the small pieces of infrastructure that make an empty road readable.
---

The road crosses the basin in a line so direct it seems drawn rather than built. At noon, the next rise hangs above the asphalt and refuses to come closer.

What makes the distance understandable are the modest things: a numbered marker, a patched fence, a stand of tamarisk around a well. Each gives the eye a scale and the traveler a sequence.

By late afternoon, the light turns the eastern ridges from brown to violet. The road finally looks finite again.
