---
title: A Night Crossing in Three Sounds
date: 2026-07-29
location: San Pedro Channel
description: Diesel rhythm, rigging in crosswind, and the low conversation of a harbor after midnight.
---

After the cabin lights dim, the crossing can be measured by sound. First comes the even pressure of the engines. Then the rigging begins to answer a crosswind. Near the far shore, harbor radio arrives in clipped fragments.

The city is visible for the entire passage, but scale disappears at night. A bright window might be a mile away or ten. The wake, briefly white behind the stern, is the only reliable measure of motion.
