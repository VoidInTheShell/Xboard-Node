title: About the notebook
date: 2026-01-01
description: Pacific Atlas is a small independent record of western landscapes and the systems that cross them.
---

Pacific Atlas collects concise observations made in transit: weather changing over a pass, a harbor beginning its day, a roadside structure doing an unnoticed job.

The project is intentionally small. Entries are published irregularly, edited for clarity, and kept free of advertising or tracking scripts.
